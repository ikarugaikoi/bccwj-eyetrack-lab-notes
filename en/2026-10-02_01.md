## 2. Purpose and overview

Five main-study sessions were conducted in the order BA, CD, DC, BA, AB. The first session (BA) and fifth session (AB) completed both halves, totaling twenty passages and eighty pages of main-task reading. The second and third sessions failed validation before practice and did not reach the practice text. The fourth session completed practice but failed validation before the first half and did not reach any main-task text.

Background questionnaires were completed. Follow-up language-proficiency testing remains scheduled for approximately two weeks later. Twelve recordings were saved on this date.

## 5. Observations and issues

**Recalibration before the first half of BA in the first session.** After the first attempt, the operator said that the calibration results were being checked, and the participant moved away from the chin rest. The operator observed this and recalibrated. Reading began after the second attempt passed the final validation. Recalibration was prompted by leaving the chin rest, rather than Accuracy exceeding the criterion.

**Accidental single-point recalibration in the second session.** During the third operation at 14:54:32, the operator intended to select Recalibrate all points but accidentally selected the middle-right point `(0.9, 0.5)` in the nine-point calibration, changing the button to Recalibrate 1 point. Only that point was collected again, followed by a complete four-point validation. The recording therefore contained four full-point calibrations, one single-point recalibration, and five four-point validations. The interval from the end of validation for the third full-point calibration to the start of the fourth full-point calibration was approximately 2 minutes 33 seconds. The final validation still failed, and no practice text was presented.

**Sessions ending after failed validation.** Final right-eye worst-point Accuracy was 1.439° before practice in the second session, 1.612° before practice in the third session, and 1.496° before the first half in the fourth session, all exceeding the 1.00° criterion. The second and third sessions did not reach practice reading, and the fourth session contains no main-task reading data.

**Two black-dot timeouts and continuation recordings in the fifth session.** After A01–A04 were completed in the first half, the black-dot check before A05 timed out, with a presentation duration of approximately 33.533 seconds. After B01–B02 were completed in the second half, the check before B03 timed out after approximately 34.467 seconds. Both recordings were stopped because of black-dot timeouts; **neither the A05 nor the B03 passage text had yet been presented at the respective interruption**. Following breaks and recalibration, the remaining passages were completed using the A05 and B03 continuation projects, respectively. Each passage text was presented only once.

Reminders were given according to the established rule for the two practice black-dot checks exceeding ten seconds in the fourth session and the approximately eighteen-second check before the first passage of the first half in the fifth session.

## 6. Data storage and quality checks

The twelve recordings and quality-check materials have been saved, exported, and archived. The right eye was selected in advance according to the established criterion. All main-task recordings in the first session (BA) and fifth session (AB) passed the final right-eye validation. Each session's ten passages and forty pages reached 80% valid-time coverage and are retained as candidate data; no main-task pages were excluded for insufficient coverage.

The second, third, and fourth sessions contain no main-task reading data. Their existing calibration and practice records remain archived. Practice data are excluded from the preregistered primary analysis.

Nine-point calibration and four-point validation were used. Acceptance required mean four-point Accuracy ≤0.90° and worst-point Accuracy ≤1.00° for the preselected eye; SD was not part of the acceptance criterion. Single-point recalibration and full-point calibrations in the second session are counted separately, and all five validation records are retained.
