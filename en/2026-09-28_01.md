## 5. Observations and issues

**The supporting script could not complete its check.** The script opened, but clicking “Start a new check” (displayed as “开始新一轮检查”) produced “Unable to determine” (displayed as “无法判定”). The operator then misread the left-eye validation values and continued the task on that basis. The specific cause of the script failure has not yet been identified.

**Misreading validation results in the AB workflow.** The final right-eye worst-point Accuracy in the recording that completed the practice set was 1.037°, and the upper-right point for the right eye in AB H1 was 1.130°. Both exceeded the 1.00° threshold, yet reading proceeded in both recordings. The corresponding left-eye values met the criteria.

**The face mask affected eye detection.** In one session, the eyes were difficult to detect during calibration and validation, and the eye-position indicators disappeared from the monitoring screen. The operator's self-test showed normal detection. On-site troubleshooting identified the mask as affecting detection: removing it restored detection, and subsequent validation passed. Difficulty with the practice black-dot checks nevertheless continued. The eye tracker is positioned along the lower edge of the monitor and views the eyes from below, so the upper edge of a mask may obstruct eye imaging or the near-infrared light path from that direction.

**Timing of stops at the black dot.** A smartphone stopwatch was used on site. The operator explained that waits exceeded 30 seconds because the exit key had not yet been pressed. Subsequent review of the records found eight practice black-dot presentations lasting more than 30 seconds. Seven were followed by stopping the recording. In the remaining case, the first practice passage appeared after approximately 41 seconds; after reading and answering the question, the recording was exited at the second passage's black dot. The longest black-dot wait was approximately 64 seconds.

**Keypresses during black-dot presentation.** Space keypresses were recorded during two black-dot checks in AB H2. The operator confirmed that the black-dot screen cannot be advanced by a keypress: these were keypresses only, and the black-dot checks were not skipped using the keyboard.

## 7. Lessons learned

When the supporting script displays “Unable to determine,” the four Accuracy values for the selected eye still require manual verification; that status must not be treated as a pass. Check the text legend and the preselected eye when assessing the results to avoid confusing left and right eyes.

Create a calibration acceptance checklist that explicitly identifies the selected eye. For the right eye selected in this session, include a checkbox labelled “Four right-eye Accuracy values checked.” Tick it after each check and record the mean and worst-point values.

Before the experiment, check for potential obstructions to eye detection, including the upper edge of a face mask. If eye-position detection is lost, check for obstructions and reconfirm eye detection and validation results.
