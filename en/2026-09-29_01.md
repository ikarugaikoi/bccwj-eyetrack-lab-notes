## 5. Observations and issues

**Recalibration during DC practice.** After the first attempt, the operator noticed a large deviation at one peripheral point, although overall calibration accuracy appeared reasonably good. The operator suspected that the participant might not yet have reacted at the start. Given that this was still practice and the formal task had not begun, the operator chose to recalibrate once more to help practice proceed smoothly. Final validation in the second attempt passed.

**Black-dot timeout and continuation at DC C05.** After C01–C04 were completed in the original H2 recording, there was a prolonged wait at the C05 black-dot check. Once the stopwatch showed more than 30 seconds, the operator first verbally asked the participant to pause and then exited the recording. The file recorded a black-dot presentation lasting 36.850 s. **Neither the C05 text nor its comprehension question had been presented.** After a break, the C05 continuation project was used. Two calibration attempts were made, and final validation passed. The black-dot check passed in approximately 1.117 s, after which the C05 text and comprehension question were completed. The passage text was presented only once.

**Right-eye confirmation.** The “Confirm right eye” checklist was used and ticked on the day. The supporting judgment script was not used.

**Consultation after the experiment.** After the experiment, a teacher involved in eye-tracking research happened to be present. The operator asked this teacher to review the experimental design and discuss possible reasons why three sessions on September 28 had not completed formal reading. The teacher suggested raising the monitor and tilting the screen slightly forward rather than keeping it perpendicular to the floor, in the hope of improving sampling. The teacher also suggested positioning the eyes slightly above the centre of the eye-position box in Tobii Pro Lab, considering that this might provide more accurate sampling than positioning them at the exact centre. These suggestions were received after the experiment; their benefits remain to be checked in subsequent trials.

## 6. Data retention and quality checks

All seven recordings and derived review materials are retained in the archive. Nine-point calibration and four-point validation were used. The validation Accuracy criteria for the selected right eye were a mean across four points ≤0.90° and a worst-point value ≤1.00°. Final saved right-eye validation passed in all seven recordings.

Right-eye valid-time coverage reached 80% for all 10 passages and 40 pages in BA and all 10 passages and 31 pages in DC. All formal passages were presented completely and only once. They are currently retained as candidate data pending final quality checks. On-site information is still needed to confirm whether the C05 continuation project had been successfully test-run before use.

Practice data are excluded from the formal analysis. Detailed metrics and reconstructed estimates of earlier validation results are retained separately as internal materials.

## 7. Lessons learned

The “Confirm right eye” checklist was used to support manual interpretation of the results. Records of interruptions and continuation should state whether the passage text had already been presented, so that the restart point and any repeated reading can be checked.

After the day's experiment, a teacher involved in eye-tracking research happened to be present. The operator asked this teacher to review the experimental design and discuss possible reasons why three sessions on September 28 had not completed formal reading. The teacher suggested raising the monitor and tilting it slightly forward rather than keeping it perpendicular to the floor, and positioning the eyes slightly above the centre of the eye-position box in Tobii Pro Lab, in the hope of improving sampling. Subsequent trials could examine these three positioning suggestions from the discussion, recording the actual setup alongside eye-detection stability, validation results, and whether the black-dot checks pass, and comparing performance before and after adjustment.
