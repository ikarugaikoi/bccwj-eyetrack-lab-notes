## 2. Purpose and overview

Four main-study sessions were conducted in the order BA, CD, AB, BA. In the first session, B01 and B02 were read across separate recordings. During an additional attempt later that day, the second-half continuation project was opened by mistake, and A03–A05 were read. The second and third sessions completed both halves, while the fourth failed validation before practice. Thirteen recordings were saved, and three sessions have formal reading candidate data.

All four participants completed the background questionnaire. Formal reading data were collected in the first three sessions, and follow-up language-proficiency testing is scheduled for approximately two weeks later. The fourth session produced no formal reading data, so no follow-up language-proficiency testing is planned.

## 5. Observations and issues

**Failed black-dot checks and first-half continuation in the first session.** After B01 was completed, the black-dot check before B02 lasted approximately 34.450 seconds without advancing to the text, and the recording was stopped. The first B02 continuation recording also stopped at the black-dot stage, after approximately 35.483 seconds. After another recalibration, B02 was completed, and recording stopped at the black-dot check before B03 after approximately 34.083 seconds. B03 text had not yet appeared.

**Wrong second-half project opened during an additional attempt in the first session.** After the other sessions that day had ended, the first session was attempted again. The operator intended to continue the first half from B03–B05 but opened `D1080_BA_H2_A03_ZH`. After right-eye validation passed, A03–A05 were read in full. The operator then switched to `D1080_BA_H1_B03_ZH`, but validation still failed after four attempts. Final right-eye mean Accuracy was 0.9905°, with a worst point of 1.735°. No B03–B05 text was presented.

The actual formal reading order was B01 → B02 → A03 → A04 → A05; B03–B05 and A01–A02 were not presented. Opening the wrong project is recorded as a procedural deviation. A03–A05 were each read completely for the first time, passed validation and coverage checks, and are retained as candidate data.

**Fourth session ended after failed validation.** Validation before practice still failed after four attempts, with final right-eye worst-point Accuracy of 3.959°. Neither practice nor formal reading began.

**Accidental keypress during reading.** In the third session, Q was accidentally pressed on page A_14 of A03, without stopping or asking the operator a question. Q did not advance the page; Space was subsequently pressed to continue. This accidental keypress is not grounds for exclusion, and the whole A03 passage is retained as candidate data.

## 6. Data storage and quality checks

The thirteen recordings and quality-check materials have been saved, exported, and archived. The first session retains B01, B02, and A03–A05, totaling five passages and nineteen pages. The second retains ten passages and thirty-one pages, and the third retains ten passages and thirty-nine pages. In total, there are twenty-five passages and eighty-nine pages of candidate data. The fourth session has no formal reading data.

Page A_10 in the third session had right-eye valid-time coverage of 77.71% and was excluded. Its parent passage A02 had whole-passage coverage of 86.77% and was retained. The wrong-project incident and actual presentation order in the first session remain recorded; A03–A05 are not relabeled as the first-half B03–B05. Comprehension-question scores are descriptive only and are not used as an exclusion criterion.

Nine-point calibration and four-point validation were used, with the right eye fixed as the selected eye. Acceptance required mean four-point Accuracy ≤0.90° and worst-point Accuracy ≤1.00°; SD was not part of the acceptance criterion. Each recording allows up to three initial attempts. If all fail, a break of at least one minute is followed by one final attempt. Practice data are excluded from the formal analysis.
