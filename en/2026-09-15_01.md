## 2. Purpose and overview

## 5. Observations and issues

Prolonged waits were observed during some black-dot fixation checks, and the operator verbally reminded participants to keep looking at the black dot. A possible connection with spectacle lenses was an on-site hypothesis; the cause has not been verified.

After reviewing the raw data and experiment records, the operator confirmed that three recalibrations in the AB workflow had resulted from mistaking Precision (SD) for Accuracy:

| Stage | Point whose value was misread | Precision (SD) |
|---|---|---:|
| AB practice, attempt 1 | Right eye, upper left | 1.240° |
| AB H1, attempt 2 | Left eye, lower left | 1.066° |
| AB H2, attempt 1 | Left eye, upper left | 1.572° |

During the first calibration attempt in AB H1, the participant reported that the screen briefly flashed while the operator was switching screens or applications. Formal reading had not yet begun, and calibration was then repeated. The operator was recording the screen with OBS and using another monitor to observe the participant's screen. The operator suspected a connection with operating OBS, but the specific cause has not been confirmed.

Subsequent review found that the worst point in the final right-eye validation for both BA H1 and BA H2 exceeded the predetermined limit, although the experiment had proceeded. The operator later recalled mistaking the left-eye values marked in green for right-eye values and therefore judging the results as passing.

BA H2 proceeded to the task after the third attempt. The operation log contains no record of a fourth attempt following a break of at least one minute.

## 6. Data retention and quality checks

All six recordings ended normally, and all materials were retained in the archive. BA H1 and H2 were excluded from the primary eye-movement analysis because validation of the selected right eye did not meet the criteria. The corresponding recruitment slot remains counted among those already recruited, with no replacement recruitment.

The validation acceptance criteria and recalibration procedure planned for the day were:

- The selected eye was fixed as the right eye. Mean Accuracy across the four validation points had to be ≤0.90°.
- Accuracy at the worst of the four points had to be ≤1.00°.
- Up to three initial attempts were allowed per recording, including the first attempt. If all three failed, a break of at least one minute was to be followed by one final attempt, for a maximum of four attempts in total.

Final right-eye worst-point Accuracy was 1.014° for BA H1 and 1.334° for BA H2. Pro Lab displayed these values as 1.01° and 1.33°, respectively; both exceeded 1.00°.

## 7. Lessons learned

Check the software's text legend and the selected eye, record Accuracy for each of the four validation points, and then check the mean and the worst point to avoid confusing the eyes. If the third attempt still fails, follow the planned break and final-attempt procedure.

Distinguish Accuracy from Precision (SD). The predetermined accuracy thresholds apply to Accuracy; SD is recorded for diagnostic purposes only.
