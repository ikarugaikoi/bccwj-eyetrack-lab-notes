## 2. Purpose and overview

Pilot sessions were conducted with two participants. Both followed the ZH (Chinese instructions) workflow: practice A → formal AB H1 → formal AB H2. Neither session had a program interruption, all black-dot fixation checks were passed normally, and the data were saved and exported. The criterion used on site was that both calibration and validation errors had to be below 1°. This criterion needs to be explicitly documented in the procedure, key operations explained in advance, and start, end, and break times recorded in full.

The monitor model was confirmed to be Dell P2425H. It was used at 1920 × 1080 and 60 Hz, with the brightness setting fixed at 90% and an eye-to-screen distance of 56 cm.

## 5. Observations and issues

1. **The calibration acceptance criterion needs to be explicitly documented.** The criterion used on site was that both calibration and validation errors had to be below 1°. It needs to be written into the procedure, and its suitability reviewed against previous research and the present experimental setup.
2. **Looking down to find the keys.** The first participant looked down to find the keys. Participants should place their hands on the keyboard before starting.
3. **The page-advance instruction needs emphasis.** During practice, a participant asked after reading the first page whether they should press the space bar when they finished reading.
4. **The viewing-distance calculation used a rounded intermediate value.** The 56 cm used in this session was converted from the 55 cm specified for the EIZO CS2400R, but that 55 cm had itself already been rounded. The experimenter recalculated directly from the conditions of the EIZO FlexScan EV2116W used in the previous study (21.5 inches, 1920 × 1080), obtaining approximately 55.3 cm. The distance will be adjusted next time.
5. **Calibration operation with two screens.** In this setup, the calibration button appeared on the monitoring screen beside the participant screen, rather than on the participant screen itself. The operator could click it directly on the monitoring screen. It was also confirmed that OBS can be used to monitor the participant screen in real time.

## 6. Data storage and quality checks

Data from both sessions were saved and exported, and the black-dot fixation checks were passed normally.

The calibration acceptance criterion used on site was that both calibration and validation errors had to be below 1°. For the second participant, calibration was performed twice during practice and twice during the start stage of the first formal half; in both cases, the first attempt had too large an error. A total of three calibrations were performed before the second formal half. Exact times will be checked against the recording materials.

## 7. Lessons learned

1. Explicitly document the calibration and validation acceptance criteria in the procedure, and record whether each calibration attempt passes.
2. Explain the key positions in advance, have participants place their hands on the keyboard, and emphasize that they should press the space bar to advance after finishing a page.
3. Calculate viewing distance directly from the original display conditions of the previous study, avoiding repeated use of rounded intermediate values.
4. Record experiment start, end, and break times on site.
5. Prepare alcohol wipes and regular tissues for wiping the chin rest.
