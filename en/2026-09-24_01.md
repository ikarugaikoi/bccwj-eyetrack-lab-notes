## 5. Observations and issues

**Misreading the CD H1 validation results.** Subsequent review found that final validation for the selected right eye had a mean Accuracy of 1.40875° and a worst-point Accuracy of 3.002°. Both exceeded the thresholds, yet the task had proceeded. After reviewing the results, the operator confirmed having mistaken the left-eye values for right-eye values at the time.

**Accidental Space press and continuation of DC H2.** An accidental Space press was reported during the first passage, and that passage was declared invalid on site. The original recording was exited during the black-dot fixation check for the second passage. **The text of the second passage had not yet been presented.** Calibration was then repeated using the C02 project, and the remaining four passages were completed starting from the second passage. The C02 project had been prepared and test-run in advance, specifically to allow the workflow to resume from passages not yet read after an interruption.

**Reminders during black-dot fixation checks.** The operator used a stopwatch and followed the rule of giving one reminder when the wait exceeded 10 seconds. Subsequent readings from the data files showed two waits of 10.050 s and 23.833 s in DC H1; all other recorded black-dot checks lasted less than 10 seconds. The second check in the original DC H2 recording lasted 4.533 s, after which the recording was exited.

## 6. Data retention and quality checks

All eight recordings and associated materials were retained in the archive. Nine-point calibration and four-point validation were used, with the selected eye fixed as the right eye. The acceptance criteria were mean Accuracy across the four validation points ≤0.90° and worst-point Accuracy ≤1.00°. Up to three initial attempts were allowed per recording. If all failed, a break of at least one minute was followed by one final attempt, for a maximum of four attempts in total.

Calibration counts include the initial attempts, for a total of 13 attempts:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CD | 1 | 1 | 1 | — |
| DC | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Terminated after failed calibration | 4 | — | — | — |

Data were handled as follows:

- **CD H1:** Excluded from the primary eye-movement analysis because right-eye validation did not meet the criteria.
- **CD H2:** Final validation passed, but right-eye valid-time coverage was below 80% for all five passages and all 15 pages. All were excluded from the primary eye-movement analysis.
- **DC:** The first passage of H2 and its corresponding comprehension question were declared invalid. The five H1 passages and four H2 continuation passages, nine in total, were retained pending final quality checks.
- **Session terminated after failed calibration:** No formal reading data were collected.

Excluded data remain archived. Sessions in which calibration had begun still count toward the recruitment quota; no replacements will be recruited.

## 7. Lessons learned

When assessing whether validation passes, check the software's text legend and the selected eye, inspect right-eye Accuracy point by point, and record the mean and worst-point values to avoid confusing the eyes again.

A supporting script is planned to check mean and worst-point Accuracy across the four right-eye validation points against the established thresholds. Its result will be double-checked against the operator's manual judgment to reduce interpretation errors when one experimenter runs the session alone.

After calibration/validation passes, valid-time coverage during actual reading still needs to be checked. Preparing and test-running continuation projects in advance makes it easier to recalibrate after a problem and resume with passages that have not yet been read.
