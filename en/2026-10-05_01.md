## 2. Purpose and overview

Four main-study sessions were conducted in the order BA, DC, AB, CD. The first session completed the first half but failed validation before the second half. The other three sessions completed both halves; the AB second half was continued from B02 after recalibration. Seventeen recordings were saved on this date.

All four participants completed the background questionnaire. Formal reading data were collected in all four sessions, including sessions with only the first half or some passages retained. Follow-up language-proficiency testing is scheduled for approximately two weeks later.

## 5. Observations and issues

**Black-dot timeouts and practice restarts in the first session.** Practice was spread across four recordings. The first three stopped at the black-dot checks before C05, D03, and C01, after waits of approximately 34.250, 36.867, and 32.433 seconds, respectively. The next passage text had not yet been presented at each stop. The fourth recording completed the entire practice set. C05 was read three times, D03 twice, and C01 once; all were practice materials.

**Failed validation before the second half in the first session.** The fourth calibration attempt before the first half passed the final right-eye validation, and B01–B05 were completed. Validation before the second half still failed after four attempts, with final right-eye worst-point Accuracy of 1.307°. No A01–A05 text was presented.

**Accidental Continue click and continuation from B02 in the third session.** In the third validation before the AB second half, right-eye lower-left Accuracy was 1.114°, exceeding the criterion. The operator intended to click Recalibrate all points but accidentally clicked Continue. The black-dot check passed quickly, and B01 began. The recording was stopped after B01 was completed, before B02 text appeared. Recalibration was then performed in the continuation project starting from B02. The first B02 continuation recording was stopped because of a black-dot timeout after approximately 33.800 seconds; B02 text had not yet appeared. A new recording was then started with recalibration, and B02–B05 were completed after validation passed. Each of B02–B05 was presented only once.

**Accidental keypress during a question.** In the fourth session, Space was accidentally pressed during the C02 question, followed by Q to answer.

## 6. Data storage and quality checks

The seventeen recordings and quality-check materials have been saved, exported, and archived. All four sessions have formal reading candidate data: five passages from the first, ten from the second, nine from the third, and ten from the fourth, totaling thirty-four passages and one hundred sixteen pages.

B01 in the third session was excluded because the right-eye validation before recording did not meet the criterion. In the second session, page D_12 had right-eye valid-time coverage of 79.07% and was excluded. Its parent passage D04 had whole-passage coverage of 94.99% and was retained. The other candidate passages and pages met the established coverage threshold.

Nine-point calibration and four-point validation were used, with the right eye fixed as the selected eye. Acceptance required mean four-point Accuracy ≤0.90° and worst-point Accuracy ≤1.00°; SD was not part of the acceptance criterion. Each recording allows up to three initial attempts. If all fail, a break of at least one minute is followed by one final attempt. Practice data are excluded from the formal analysis.
